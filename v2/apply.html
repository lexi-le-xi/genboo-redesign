FAQ

Questions parents ask

I’ve never built a robot. Is that OK?

Yes. The program starts with lab safety and the robot as a system, and every step has a clear success check before the next one. Curiosity matters more than background.

Why a humanoid, not a simpler kit?

A humanoid needs every discipline at once: mechanics to stand, electronics to power on, software to move, AI to move on its own. We use ToddlerBot, an open-source humanoid from Stanford researchers. It’s small and affordable, and its parts can be bought or 3D-printed.

What does it cost?

Fees are discussed during the interview, and need-based scholarships are available.
